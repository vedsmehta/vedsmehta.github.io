# Dormammu

Built at SpeedHacks (an a16z speedrun hackathon) on October 11, 2026 with Aarav
Kothari, Aksh Parekh, and Shubh Shah. Dormammu takes a single request — call
around and find the best deal on X — and fans it out into several concurrent
AI-driven phone calls, each preceded by a research stage that figures out who
to call, what to ask, and what counts as a good outcome before dialing.

Auth and data run on Supabase, the frontend is Next.js, and call analytics feed
into Keewano so we could see win rates and outcomes across calls live during the
demo.
